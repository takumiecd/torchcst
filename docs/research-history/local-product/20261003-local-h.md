Each candidate runs in a fresh Python/CUDA process. About 5% atoms, ordinary
sigma-three (effective FP32 sigma 2.99999976), SGD displacement lr 1e-4 plus
polar activity update. Include decode, ordering/packing, full-domain norms,
local forward, dX, all atom gradients and update. CUDA Graph median of three
rounds, milliseconds:

This is the same **CST atom model and polar update** on every path. Materialized
W is not a free-weight nn.Linear training step. Ready-W forward is recorded
separately and excludes generation/backward/update. Mixed-width cases span
roughly sigma 1..16 and show the same trend; full results are retained in JSON.
These local experiments do not establish the original 1024/8192 L4 objective.

The packed fused path's forward including preparation is 0.112865/0.114401/
0.155648 ms. Its prepared core alone is 0.004465/0.005919/0.017183 ms. Preparation
clearly matters. Sorting currently costs more than it saves at these sizes.
The fused/saved differences in total time are small; one run is not evidence
of a universal winner. Compiler reports show zero spill slots for all measured
custom forward/backward variants; no cache-hit or DRAM counter was measured.

## Scope and next optimization

- Only the small local primitive is implemented; no outer GEMM or production
  Strip/Torus integration.
- Packing is contiguous SoA; canonical atom gathers occur during preparation.
  Forward scans fixed 16/32-atom blocks. Rho buckets order atoms but do not yet
  specialize loop lengths or ensure blocks do not cross bucket boundaries.
- Profile evaluation still touches the whole local tile, including zero-valued
  positions. This baseline has not yet exploited support width to shorten
  contiguous input/output loops. Full normalization scans are bounded to 128
  sites per side; local counts are bounded to 64.
- A single small tile uses few thread blocks. Prepared-core timing on an
  otherwise idle L4 does not establish throughput of many concurrent tiles.
- The immediate optimization target is preparation cost and support-aware
  local loops; adding global W or a large outer matrix schedule is not this
  milestone.
